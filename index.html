The Problem: A Broken Trust Infrastructure

Africa's transition to digital is hampered by antiquated verification methods.

  • Paper-Based Friction: Physical wet signatures are slow, costly (printing/courier), and delay critical transactions.
  • High Cost of Compliance: Global tools like DocuSign are priced in dollars, making them unaffordable for African SMEs.
  • Trust Deficit: A lack of localized KYC leads to higher fraud risks and lower enforceability in local courts.
  • Regulatory Gap: Foreign tools often fail to meet specific local mandates (e.g., NIN integration in Nigeria).

The Solution: INKLESS

Secure, Localized E-Signatures

Inkless is a mobile-first ecosystem for authentication, authorization, and witnessing of documents.

  • 01. Identity verification via localized KYC (NIN, Biometrics).
  • 02. Acceptance by courts, legal system and business community.
  • 03. AI-powered document review to flag risky clauses.
  • 04. Blockchain notarization for incorruptible audit trails.
  • 05. Affordable pricing model tailored for African markets.
